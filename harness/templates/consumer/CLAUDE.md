## House style

GUI.Forms code follows one house style, and so should yours. Read
`.devkit/HOUSE_STYLE.md` in full before writing code; its sections are
ordered by importance, most important first. `python3 build.py check`
checks the mechanical part of it. Where a check and HOUSE_STYLE.md disagree,
HOUSE_STYLE.md wins.
