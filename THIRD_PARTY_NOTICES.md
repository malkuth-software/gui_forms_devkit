# Third-party notices

gui_forms_devkit's own source is MIT (`LICENSE`). Pocket Reference is MIT
too (`pocket_reference/LICENSE`, `pocket_reference/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).

Content that keeps its own licence:

| Content | Where | Licence and notice |
|---|---|---|
| GUI.Forms, its fonts, icons and third-party parts | the GUI.Forms SDK every build links; programs and packages copy its `share/licenses/GUIForms` | the SDK's `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` |
| The packaged programs' licence texts (LLVM runtime, fonts) | `programs/packaging/licenses/`, listed by `programs/packaging/licenses.json` | each text as named there |
| The app template's notices | `harness/templates/app/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` | as listed there |
| Licence texts for generated apps (MIT, PolyForm Noncommercial, proprietary) | `harness/templates/licenses/` | templates an app chooses from; not the devkit's licence |
